# Glimmerdark balance changelog

Every number below is from the full sweep of that version: all 50 delver line-ups (2, 3 and 4 players) at 10,000 games each, tactician AI in every seat, plus the strategy, luck, playtime and ability-duel runs at the same count. Playtime assumes 45 s per player turn and 10 s per Warden phase. Generated by `print/designers_notes.py` from `results/`.

## v1: First draft, exactly as written

The rules in design/rules_v1_first_draft.md, written before any simulation.

**Result (full sweep):**

- Playtime at 45 s a turn: median 48 / 41 / 38 min (2 / 3 / 4 players); 35% / 74% / 99% of games inside 30–45 min.
- Delver balance: best-to-worst spread 47.8 / 32.7 / 24.9 points; furthest from a fair share 29.0 / 20.5 / 15.3 points.
- Outside ±5 points: Mira -29.0 (2p), Gritch +18.8 (2p), Sable +7.9 (2p), Mira -20.5 (3p), Gritch +12.2 (3p), Sable +7.2 (3p), Old Quill +6.7 (3p), Mira -15.3 (4p), Old Quill +9.5 (4p), Gritch +7.8 (4p).
- First player wins 52.8% / 36.8% / 27.5% (fair 50% / 33.3% / 25%).
- Halfway leader wins 79% / 65% / 55%; lead changes per game 2.8 / 3.2 / 2.9.
- Planning AI wins 68% against greedy and 48% against cautious; greedy wins 32% against cautious (2 players).
- Crushes per game 11.0 / 11.8 / 11.7.

## v2: Shorter, less snowbally, and a reason to go deep

**Changes (data from the previous version → change):**

- **The collapse track: jokers now move the marker too (a Tremor), and the end space depends on player count (7 / 8 / 9 with 2 / 3 / 4 players) instead of 3 reshuffles for everyone.** Median playtime was 48 / 41 / 38 minutes with 2 / 3 / 4 players, and only 35% of 2-player games fitted the 30–45 minute window.
- **The Warden walks toward the delver who is winning (vault plus pack), not the one carrying the most.** The player leading at the halfway point won 79% / 65% / 55% of games. The Warden is the game's catch-up mechanism, so it should lean on the leader.
- **Deep glimmer is worth 4, not 3.** Going deep didn't pay: the cautious AI beat the greedy one 68% of the time and beat the planning AI 52%. Sitting in the shallows was the best strategy, which is dull.
- **The first player's first turn has 1 action instead of 2.** The first player won 52.8% of 2-player games (fair: 50%).
- **Mira: the first ♥ she plays to Mine each turn costs no action (hearts still match every vein).** Mira was -29.0 / -20.5 / -15.3 points from a fair share (2 / 3 / 4 players). Duelling a delver with no ability at all, Mira won 47.1% of games: letting hearts match any vein was no better than having no ability, because the scarce thing on a turn is movement, not matching.
- **Gritch: a ♠ Move takes him up to 2 chambers instead of 3.** Gritch was +18.8 / +12.2 / +7.8 points from a fair share: in a game where movement is the bottleneck, triple moves were too much.

**Result (full sweep):**

- Playtime at 45 s a turn: median 33 / 36 / 35 min (2 / 3 / 4 players); 86% / 88% / 100% of games inside 30–45 min.
- Delver balance: best-to-worst spread 9.5 / 12.8 / 12.3 points; furthest from a fair share 5.3 / 7.1 / 6.7 points.
- Outside ±5 points: Mira +5.3 (2p), Mira +7.1 (3p), Pip & Pell -5.8 (3p), Mira +6.7 (4p), Pip & Pell -5.7 (4p), Gritch -5.3 (4p).
- First player wins 49.1% / 33.3% / 24.9% (fair 50% / 33.3% / 25%).
- Halfway leader wins 68% / 56% / 48%; lead changes per game 2.9 / 3.4 / 3.2.
- Planning AI wins 60% against greedy and 60% against cautious; greedy wins 50% against cautious (2 players).
- Crushes per game 8.9 / 10.8 / 12.1.

## v3: Retuning the delvers

**Changes (data from the previous version → change):**

- **Gritch: back to 3 chambers per ♠, but he can't pass through the Gate (he may stop there), and his pack holds 4 instead of 5.** At 2 chambers Gritch was -1.1 / -4.8 / -5.3. Speed is his identity, so it came back, and the cost moved to how much he can haul and how easily he banks.
- **Hulda: a high ♣ (7–10, or the Jack, which matches as a 10) played as Pilfer takes 2 glimmer instead of 1.** Hulda was -2.7 / -4.0 / -3.6.
- **Pip & Pell: twice per turn instead of once, and a suited run (same suit, next rank up or down) counts as well as a pair.** Pip & Pell were -4.2 / -5.8 / -5.7; pairs in a five-card hand are too rare to lean on.
- **Old Quill: his Jack Shortcut moves 4 chambers instead of 3.** Old Quill was -1.4 / +2.8 / +4.7. Win rates are shared, so raising three delvers pulls the other three down; Quill was already below par with 2 players and would have sunk further, so he got a small boost too.
- **No change to Mira or Sable.** They led: Mira +5.3 / +7.1 / +6.7, Sable +4.0 / +4.8 / +3.2. Rather than weaken abilities that play well, the trailing delvers were brought up to meet them.
- **The first player's short first turn now applies only with 3 or 4 players.** The first player won 49.1% / 33.3% / 24.9% with 2 / 3 / 4 players (fair: 50% / 33.3% / 25%). With 2 players the one-action first turn over-corrected; with 3 and 4 it was spot on.

**Result (full sweep):**

- Playtime at 45 s a turn: median 33 / 36 / 35 min (2 / 3 / 4 players); 81% / 87% / 100% of games inside 30–45 min.
- Delver balance: best-to-worst spread 8.0 / 2.0 / 3.4 points; furthest from a fair share 4.5 / 1.2 / 1.9 points.
- Outside ±5 points: none.
- First player wins 50.1% / 33.4% / 24.8% (fair 50% / 33.3% / 25%).
- Halfway leader wins 69% / 56% / 48%; lead changes per game 2.8 / 3.3 / 3.1.
- Planning AI wins 61% against greedy and 62% against cautious; greedy wins 52% against cautious (2 players).
- Crushes per game 8.8 / 10.6 / 11.9.

## v4: One clean rule for Hulda

**Changes (data from the previous version → change):**

- **Hulda: face-card ♣ count as high too, so any ♣ of 7 or more takes 2.** In v3 the J♣ counted as high (a Jack matches as a 10) but the Q♣ and K♣ did not, which was hard to remember and harder to print. Hulda was -0.9 / -0.4 / +0.2 before the change.

**Result (full sweep):**

- Outside ±5 points: none.
- First player wins 49.9% / 33.3% / 24.7% (fair 50% / 33.3% / 25%).
- Halfway leader wins 69% / 56% / 48%; lead changes per game 2.8 / 3.3 / 3.1.
- Planning AI wins 61% against greedy and 62% against cautious; greedy wins 52% against cautious (2 players).
