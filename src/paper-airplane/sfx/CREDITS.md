# Paper Airplane sound effects: credits

All files are **CC0 (public domain)** from OpenGameArt.org; no attribution is required, but sources are
recorded here. Each was trimmed, converted to mono 64 kbps MP3 and peak-normalised to -3 dB. The game still
has synthesised fallbacks for every sound (see `playSound` in `src/paper-airplane.js`).
